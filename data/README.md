CSV files are generated by the scripts in `src/` and are not committed:

- `thai_gold_reprices.csv`: raw re-price history (`scrape_thai_gold.py`)
- `thai_gold_clean.csv`: cleaned history with Kalman premium (`analyze_real_data.py`)
- `market_1m.csv`, `market_1h.csv`: world gold and USD/THB (`get_market_data.py`)
